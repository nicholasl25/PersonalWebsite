Work Experience

Machine Learning Intern

Bristol Myers Squibb

Lawrenceville, NJ May to Aug 2025

At Bristol Myers Squibb, I joined a team focused on predicting pharmacokinetic parameters from molecular structure so researchers could make better decisions earlier in discovery. The larger goal was to identify stronger compounds sooner and reduce animal testing by improving confidence before trial planning.

I started by building the feature pipeline in Python, using RDKit and Mordred to generate molecular descriptors and clean representations for modeling. From there, I trained CNN and GNN models to learn structure behavior patterns and benchmarked model variants across different candidate sets.

As the models matured, I used PCA and k means clustering to check chemical space coverage and reduce blind spots across compound classes. I then ran k fold cross validation with GMFE based evaluation and reached 1.7, giving the team a stronger early screening signal for prioritizing which compounds to move forward.

Data Analytics & Forecasting Intern

The Port Authority of New York & New Jersey

New York, NY May to Aug 2024

At the Port Authority, I worked on forecasting passenger volumes at Newark, JFK, and LaGuardia to support recurring reporting and planning decisions. The team needed forecasts that were accurate enough to trust in operational conversations and frequent stakeholder updates.

I built time series pipelines in Python using SARIMAX and ensemble modeling, then segmented the workflow by airport, airline, and direction so each forecast reflected different demand patterns. I also implemented backtesting and extrapolation modes so analysts could both validate historical performance and project forward.

After the core models were stable, I wrapped the system in a Flask report application with configurable settings and export options so teams could generate updates on demand. The final pipeline delivered about 2 percent MAPE and made regular reporting faster, more consistent, and easier to share across stakeholders.
